08 / Personality

Brand Pillars

Every design decision should be legible through at least one of these lenses.

Warm

The tactile warmth of a well-made notebook. Soft backgrounds, gentle shadows, slightly warm tones. A workspace a writer would be proud to spend hours in.

Creative

Carries genuine personality, quietly. Respects the writer's headspace. Has the confidence of a trusted editor giving honest notes, not a chatbot reading from a script.

Focused

The writing comes first. Chrome, controls, and AI features retreat to the edges. The editor is the product. Everything else is scaffolding.

09 / Guidelines

Design Principles

Pattern recognition for staying on-brand.

Do

  • Use warm whites and soft off-whites
  • Keep accents desaturated and gentle
  • Use opacity for text on glass surfaces
  • Let the writing surface be the hero
  • Use smooth, unhurried transitions
  • Honor the one-color-per-action system
  • Write like a thoughtful editor

Don't

  • Use pure white backgrounds
  • Use harsh primary colors or neon
  • Add aggressive gradients or sparkles
  • Create dense, dashboard-like chrome
  • Use instant or jarring state transitions
  • Mix color associations between features
  • Use emoji in UI labels

10 / References

Inspiration

What Quillium aspires to feel like, and what it explicitly avoids.
