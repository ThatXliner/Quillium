Three paper planes fly across a bright paper sky of drifting ink dust, one ink trail splitting into three — each carrying a different draft of the same sentence: “The rain found her before the door did.”, “She stepped out into the waiting rain.”, “Rain met her at the threshold.”. Pieces of the Quillium interface accompany the flight: a revision card with version pills that swap between the three takes, and a nested revision card that swaps a phrase inside one of them.
