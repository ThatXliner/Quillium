# Manifesto

We built this for the writers who know the first version is never the only version.

For the novelist who writes the same chapter four different ways, hoping one of them is right. For the essayist who can't commit to a thesis until they've argued three of them. For the memoirist who rewrites sentences until 2am and still isn't sure they've gotten it.

Writing tools have always assumed you know where you're going. They give you a blank page and expect a straight line from start to finish. But writing isn't a straight line — it's a web of attempts, a tangle of better and worse, a conversation between drafts.

**Quillium was built for the tangle.**

We built an editor that treats non-linearity as a feature, not a problem to manage. Where AI isn't a distraction but a second voice in the room — one that annotates rather than interrupts, that responds to your text rather than pulling you away from it. Where comments, revisions, and suggestions live right where they belong: beside the words they're about.

We kept it warm because writing is personal. We kept it calm because flow is fragile. We kept it focused because the writing is the product — everything else is scaffolding.

You have something to say. We built a tool worthy of the work.
